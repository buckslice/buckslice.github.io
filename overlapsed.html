Overlapsed

Instructions

See how high you can climb! Use your masks to reveal hidden platforms and powerups. This game is best played with 2 players, one on keyboard and one on the controller, each controlling one mask! But its playable as a single player too, just click LMB to switch between your masks! If 2 player you can hold RMB or B to give the lower player low gravity so they can catch up. Have fun!
