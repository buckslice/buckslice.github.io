Overview

I worked at Intrepid Studios for 5 and a half years. I started out as a generalist designer with the rest of the associate hires helping out with the Alpha 1 launch. I then worked on the Systems team supporting the features around player built cities: Nodes, and other related systems for some time. But it wasn't long until I gravated towards the Combat team and found my home. I worked on that team through our Alpha 2 and Steam Early Access launches.

Archetype (Class) Design

I was the primary design owner of the Bard and Rogue archetypes, both of which were well received by the community for their fun, thematic gameplay and compelling mechanical interactions. I also contributed in various ways to the rest of the archetypes. I helped with the initial designs for our earlier ones and then later helped maintain implementation quality and balance of our abilities across the board.

Combat Stat Formulas / Overall Balance

I was the primary design owner for all combat stats and many core stat formulas in the game. I built and maintained the main combat stat spreadsheet, including an attribute to waterfall conversions chart, and a full combat calculator that allowed designers to configure players or NPCs with different gear sets, archetypes, and elite ranks, and see their resulting in-game stats, DPS, and TTK across levels 1-50. The sheet also contained an archetype ability analysis section that modeled costs, cooldowns, relative power, and burst vs efficiency charts to help us identify abilities that were generally too lacking or exceeding over the rest.

Beyond this I also regularly collaborated with the Economy team, managed consumable item implementation, item set bonus implentation, player XP curves, monster/elite XP rewards, and advised on gear passes and related stat updates.

Tools and Tech

I built a variety of scripts and tools to support my own and the rest of my teams work over the years. Here are some of the main ones:

Damage Meter

I built an internal damage meter tool to track hit events during combat. It worked similarly to damage meter addons in games like World of Warcraft. The system reported damage, healing, mana gained, DPS, and other combat metrics by instigator and target, and proved invaluable for playtest analysis given the team's limited BI capabilities.


Dynamic Tooltip System

I built a feature-rich text parser for our tooltip description data that could isolate various keywords and arguments and replace them with auto formatted rich text that referenced other data. The system supported tooltips for Abilities, Effects (buffs/debuffs), and Items. I'm a big proponent of "single source of truth" data management and this allowed us to iterate fast and have always up to date, informative, and accurate tooltips in the game.




Floating Damage Text System

I took ownership of reworking the damage floater system when it became clear it needed more attention. I implemented a new, customizable and optimized solution that featured dynamically spaced floaters, relative magnitude sizing, recent floater concatenation, effect application popups, and more punchy animations, overall resulting in better communication of the impactful moments in our combat.


Stat Inspector

I built an internal stat inspector tool that developers could use to inspector any target's stats or owned effects (buffs/debuffs). This tool bypassed normal data replication rules since most of these stats weren't replicated to other clients normally. The tool was used across the team to help debug issues.
