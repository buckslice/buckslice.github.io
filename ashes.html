Overview

I worked at Intrepid Studios for 5 and a half years. I started out as a generalist designer with the rest of the associate hires helping with our Alpha 1 launch. I then worked on player built cities: Nodes, and other related systems for some time, but it wasn't long until I gravated towards the Combat team and found my home. I primarily worked on that team through Alpha 2 launch and Early Access launch.

Archetype (Class) Design

I was the primary design owner of the Bard and Rogue archetypes. These archetypes were well received by the community for being fun to play with interesting and thematic interactions. I also worked on the rest of the archetypes in various ways.

Combat Stat Formulas / Overall Balance

I was the primary design owner of all the combat stats and many other stat formulas in the game. I built and managed the main combat stat spreadsheet that had a full combat calculator where you could load up a player / npc in variety of gear sets, archetypes, elite rank, etc. And get a full view of what their stats would be in game and relative DPS / TTK.

There were also tabs for each archetype ability evaluating their costs, relative power, cooldown, burst vs efficiency ratios and a variety of graphs and charts for all this data. I also managed the consumable items balance and frequenty interfaced with the Econ team and helped advise on gear updates.

Tools and Tech

Damage Meter

I built the internal damage meters that could be used to track all hit events in the game, and how much damage/healing/other was being done by various instigators and towards various targets in the game.

Stat Inspector

The stat inspector allowed devs to track any stat on any target and view their true values. Most of these things weren't replicated normally for optimization/security reasons, but this allowed us to see them anyways to help us debug issues and assess our designs.

Dynamic Tooltip System

I built a parser for our string ability description data that could find keywords and replace text with rich text data descriptions for the various tooltips in our game. It supported Abilities, Effects (buffs/debuffs), and Items. I a big proponent of "single source of truth" designs and this allowed us to iterate fast and have always up to date and informative tooltips in the game.

Floating Damage Text System

For a long time our damage floater system was lacking so I took the time to rework it. The UI team had a lot on their plate and I am of the opinion that the combat floaters are very important to the feeling of combat in general. You gotta see those big juicy numbers pop on your screen and they need to be customizable and look just right. So I implemented my own version that were also dynamically spaced and had a variety of nice features.
