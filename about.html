About Me

Hello my name is Bucky and I am an accomplished game designer with a strong technical background. I'm most passionate about combat, class design, systems, and multiplayer games. I graduated from UC Irvine in 2017 with a B.S in Computer Game Science. I worked on Ashes of Creation @ Intrepid Studios for almost 6 years before the project suddenly ended, but I am eager to start my next adventure! I love making games and have links to all my projects on the Home page. Please check out my fun and often wacky creations, enjoy your stay!
